# Changelog

All notable changes to this repository are listed here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog 1.1.0](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/). The first entry is the record tag `cos-1494`; from 1.1.0 on, the versions follow [Semantic Versioning 2.0.0](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html) and cover the code, the data and the documentation. The text of the paper changes only through [`ERRATA.md`](ERRATA.md).

This repository was recreated with a new history on 2026-10-05; the earlier release, v1.0.3 of 2026-08-01, was not the version of record and is withdrawn. It carried the research report in its state before the revision.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-10-05

### Added

- `README.md`: the two works, the abstract of the paper with four translations and the abstract of the report in five languages, the publication, the version of record, a summary of the work, the contents, the traceability data, what is public and what is not, how to reproduce the documents, the limitations, the corrections, the citation and the licences.
- `slides/`: the sources of the deck and the handout of the talk on the paper at the VIMS-Workshop 2026 on 2026-05-22, revised in October 2026 so that they agree with the revised edition of the report and with `ERRATA.md`; each revised frame says what changed. The research question of the paper and the drawing of the V-model after its Fig. 2, which the deck takes from the paper, keep the licence of the paper. The deck as presented is not archived.
- `verification/`: the traceability data of the diagnostic path, reconstructed in 2026 from Chapters 3 and 5 of the report: eight requirements and nine tests or checks with their status and the evidence each status rests on, a JSON Schema, a generated CSV view, an empty template and a data dictionary. No test has the status PASS.
- `tools/check_traceability.py`, which checks the traceability data, refuses PASS without archived primary evidence and requires the status NOT-DOCUMENTED together with the method not-tested and no date; `tools/check_metadata.py` and `tools/check_links.py`, which check the citation metadata and the links; the three refuse an unknown or surplus argument with exit status 2 and end with 2 when they could not check, and each case of the self-tests of the first two names the findings it has to produce; and the targets `slides`, `traceability` and `metadata` of the `Makefile`.
- The workflows, the correction form, `SECURITY.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `FUNDING.yml`, the opt-in pre-commit hook in `.githooks/`, which scans staged changes with `gitleaks git --staged`, the configurations of gitleaks and markdownlint, and the social card.

### Changed

- `CITATION.cff` and `codemeta.json` carry the version 1.1.0; their description names the slides and the traceability data, and `codemeta.json` names Python and the shell among the languages and Python with PyYAML and jsonschema 4.0 or later among the requirements.
- `NOTICE` and `REUSE.toml` give the licences of the files added, and `NOTICE` calls the three reports of prior work it credits unpublished, as the README does.
- `report/README.md` gives the measured overlap of the text of the report with the deposited paper and the word rule of the measurement, and dates the double build of the report 2026-10-08, after the corrections of the report listed under Fixed; at the tag it gives 2026-10-05.
- `.gitattributes` marks `docs/`, which holds the social card, as documentation.
- `report/`: the text uses one term for the rebuilt unit and one form for each board name, splits its sentences of more than 60 words outside the passages the author fixed, gives the timing of the watchdog and the polarity and slave select of the SPI link sub-subsections of their own, replaces three inflated phrases and drops an entry of its bibliography source that it does not cite; at the tag the report has the earlier text.
- `report/`: the list of references prints its dates as day, month and year, as the text writes them, and the report cites the subsection of the paper as V-E, as `ERRATA.md` does; at the tag the report has the earlier forms.
- `ERRATA.md`: the entry on the author's website under Updates describes the address printed in the author note without writing it out, since it does not open; at the tag the entry writes it out.
- `report/README.md` says that a build of the report is untagged and has no structure tree for assistive technology, which PDF/A-2b does not require; at the tag it does not say so.
- `NOTICE` and the READMEs of `publication/` and `paper/` write the dates of days in ISO form, as the README and the tables do; at the tag they write them as day, month and year.

### Fixed

- `publication/README.md` and `report/README.md` call Cologne Open Science and ePublications a publication server of TH Köln, which runs both; at the tag each calls its server the publication server of TH Köln.
- `NOTICE` says that the author took the photographs of Appendix A of the report, which it licenses under CC BY 4.0, and, like `REUSE.toml`, that all the board designs they show are a third party's, the Rev 1.0 STM32 Diagnostics Board among them. - `.chktexrc` names for each suppressed warning the constructs in these sources that trigger it, and says that none triggers warning 38 at present; at the tag the comments of warnings 1, 6, 9, 10, 12, 16, 17, 18, 25, 29, 36 and 38 describe other constructs, and those of 8, 13 and 24 only some of them. - `report/`: the summary of the evidence (Sections 5.1.3 and 6.1, Tables 5.3 and 6.1) names the two hardware changes of Section 4.6 that alter the behaviour of the module by design, and rests the deviation of the sweep at its low end on the comparison captures of the original board and in its duration on the design values set against the fixture report's capture; Sections 4.1 and 5.4.1 describe the V-model without claiming a common use they do not source; the captions of Figures 3.1 and 4.1 say that the figures are adapted from the paper and stay under its licence, and the caption of Figure 5.2 says how it relates to Fig. 2 of the paper; reference 3 has the title and type of the work report's title page; Table B.1 writes the converter Y23 as the bill of materials does; Appendix B.2 says that the layer numbers of the stack-up are Eagle's; Section 5.1.1 states how the periods of the raw export of the sweep were evaluated. - `report/README.md` names the board designs of Rev 0.0 and Rev 1.0 among the material that the licence of the report excludes, as `NOTICE` and `REUSE.toml` do, and says that the title page lists the Rev 0.0 designs only; at the tag it names the Rev 0.0 designs only.
