# Contributing

This repository holds finished work: the paper "Ensuring Functional Equivalence in Retrofitted Anti-Skid Systems for European Public Transportation: A Hybrid Two-Layer Verification Methodology" by Mert Torun as deposited, the author's corrected manuscript of it, the research report from which it derives, the slides of the talk on it and the traceability data of the diagnostic path. It is not developed further and takes no feature contributions.

## Corrections

Report an error in the paper, the report, the slides, the traceability data or the documentation through the issue form **Correction**, with the location, the quoted text, the proposed correction and the evidence, after checking [`ERRATA.md`](ERRATA.md). A matter that should not be public goes by e-mail to [info@mtorun0x7cd.com](mailto:info@mtorun0x7cd.com).

The text of a work changes only by errata: the deposited paper and the sources at the tag `cos-1494` stay as they are, a confirmed error of the paper is added to `ERRATA.md`, and a change to the corrected manuscript in `paper/` names its erratum. The research report keeps the text of its revised edition until its deposit; after the deposit, its errors are listed in `ERRATA.md` as well.

## Pull requests

A pull request that changes the deposited file, the text of the paper or of the report, or the traceability data beyond a confirmed correction is not merged. Security reports follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
